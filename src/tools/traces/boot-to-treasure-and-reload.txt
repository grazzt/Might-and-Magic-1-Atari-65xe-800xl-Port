# Original v2: treasure, a save, and the saved game loaded again after the
# machine has been switched off. The six supplied characters go down the stairs
# under Sorpigal, meet three gremlins on the way to 4,7 and choose to attack,
# win, search, open the iron chest they find (a gas trap, gold and a halberd),
# walk back and sign in at the inn, which saves. Then the machine is started
# again: the inn shows the saved characters, and the party goes out and walks.
