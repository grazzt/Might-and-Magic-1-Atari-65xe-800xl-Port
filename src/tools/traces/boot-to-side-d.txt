# Original v2: boot side A, swap to B, leave Sorpigal onto side C, walk south
# through sector EJ into sector EP and enter the portal at 13,13, which leads
# to an area on side D. The route was computed from the three area files'
# movement grids and their neighbour fields.
# NOTE 2026-10-13: it never gets through. A wandering group of dinolizards meets the
# party at the portal square and the keys after `mark portal` do nothing, so no
# read from side D happens (the C64's own recording shows the same). The marks
# `portal`, `insert-d` and `side-d` are all that encounter. Side D is read by
# boot-to-direct-if, -eh and -dp.
until $41ab          # title keyboard poll
mark title
key 5f               # left arrow: the game's Escape
wait $1000000        # this prompt polls the keyboard itself, not through $0E3B
mark insert-b
